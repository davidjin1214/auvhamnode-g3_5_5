## Scope

- Dataset/context: `oc`, `nominal_train`
- Seeds: `42, 43, 44`
- Noise profiles: `clean`, `nominal_eval`, `degraded_eval`
- Horizons: `10s`, `30s`, `60s`
- Scenario groups: `PRBS`, `CHIRP`, `OU`
- Purpose: fast lookup of per-model, per-seed, per-noise, per-scenario rollout results without reopening the raw log

## How To Read

- `overall` tables are aggregated across all scenarios for one seed and one noise profile.
- `scenario` tables expose scenario-specific results at the same seed and noise profile.
- `completion` is the fraction of trajectories that survive to the horizon in that table row.
- `pred_div` is the final rollout-level prediction divergence rate for the same group.
- `nan` means the corresponding seed/profile failed so early that the horizon statistic is not defined.
